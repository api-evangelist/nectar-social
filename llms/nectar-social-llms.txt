# Nectar Social
> Nectar is an AI-powered social OS that helps brands manage community engagement, social listening, influencer tracking, and commerce attribution.

## About
Nectar is the AI-powered social teammate for modern brands. Founded in 2023, Nectar helps D2C and enterprise brands turn social engagement into measurable revenue.

## Key Features
- Community Management: AI-powered response management across Instagram, TikTok, and X
- Social Listening: Real-time cross-channel monitoring and competitor tracking
- Performance Analytics: KPI dashboards, post-level insights, and attribution
- Influencer Tracking: Unified creator data hub and workflow management
